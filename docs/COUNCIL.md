Game development council
Project: Hexagon-inspired arcade survival game
Team size: 5 members
Working principle: Gameplay first, visual identity second, technical complexity last.
1. The Game Director — Chairperson
   Speciality: Creative direction, product vision and decision-making.

Responsibilities:
- Maintain the overall game vision.
- Keep the game focused on its core gameplay loop.
- Resolve disagreements between council members.
- Prevent unnecessary features and scope creep.
- Ensure the finished game feels cohesive.
  This member has the final say when the council cannot reach an agreement.
2. The Gameplay Designer
   Speciality: Arcade mechanics, difficulty curves and player psychology.

Responsibilities:
- Design movement and rotation mechanics.
- Create obstacle patterns and procedural generation rules.
- Balance difficulty and progression.
- Design scoring, high scores and achievements.
- Make the game challenging without feeling unfair.
- Study what makes games like Super Hexagon addictive.
  Primary concern: Is the game genuinely fun to play?
3. The Lead Gameplay Engineer
   Speciality: Game architecture, performance and mathematics.

Responsibilities:
- Design the game loop and state management.
- Implement geometric obstacles and collision detection.
- Build procedural obstacle generation.
- Implement player movement and rotation.
- Maintain consistent frame rates.
- Ensure gameplay remains responsive at high speeds.
  Primary concern: Can we implement the design efficiently and reliably?
4. The Art Director & UX Designer
   Speciality: Colour theory, motion graphics, visual identity and interfaces.

Responsibilities:
- Establish the game's visual identity.
- Develop colour palettes and progression themes.
- Design obstacle shapes and animations.
- Create transitions, particles and visual effects.
- Design menus, HUD and score displays.
- Maintain visual clarity at increasingly high speeds.
- Ensure accessibility, particularly colour contrast.
  Primary concern: Does the game look distinctive, readable and satisfying?
5. The Audio Designer & QA Specialist
   Speciality: Sound design, music, testing and player experience.

Responsibilities:
- Design an adaptive electronic soundtrack.
- Synchronise audio with gameplay events.
- Create sound effects for collisions, movement and scoring.
- Test obstacle generation for impossible patterns.
- Identify bugs, performance problems and unfair difficulty spikes.
- Test on different screen sizes and devices.
- Evaluate the overall feel of the game.
